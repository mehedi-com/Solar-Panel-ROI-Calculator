Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this solar calculator?

This calculator provides estimates based on average solar panel performance and typical electricity rates. For a precise assessment, consult with a local solar installer who can evaluate your specific roof conditions, shading, and local incentives.

What factors affect solar panel ROI?

Key factors include: your local electricity rates, available sunlight, roof orientation and angle, system cost, available incentives and tax credits, and future electricity rate increases. The calculator accounts for many of these variables.

How long do solar panels typically last?

Most solar panels come with 25-year warranties and can continue producing electricity beyond that timeframe, though at slightly reduced efficiency. Inverter components may need replacement after 10-15 years.
